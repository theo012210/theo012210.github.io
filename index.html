How to play

Welcome! Follow these steps to get the most out of this rhythm listening challenge:

  • Pick a difficulty level that suits you and adjust the note frequency sliders if you want to bias certain rhythms.
  • Press Play to hear the rhythm. You will get two full bars of prep beats before the question rhythm starts.
  • Compare the playback with the rendered notation options and click the one that matches the rhythm you heard.
  • You have three attempts; new questions will appear automatically after you guess correctly or use all tries.
